response 1 (EGR1) gene fluorescence in-situ hybridization (FISH) test system for specimen 
82

II. Scope 
92
The scope of this document is limited to the devices identified in 21 CFR 864.1870 as: 
93
An early growth response 1 (EGR1) gene fluorescence in-situ hybridization (FISH) test 
94
system for specimen characterization is a device intended to detect the EGR1 probe target on 
95
chromosome 5q in bone marrow specimens from patients with acute myeloid leukemia 
96
(AML) or myelodysplastic syndrome (MDS).  The assay results are intended to be 
97
interpreted only by a qualified pathologist or cytogeneticist.  These devices do not include 
98

A. Content and Format of an Abbreviated 510(k) 
111
112
An Abbreviated 510(k) submission must include the required elements identified in 21 CFR 
113
807.87, including the proposed labeling for the device sufficient to describe the device, its 
114
intended use, and the directions for its use.  In an Abbreviated 510(k) for this device, FDA 
115
may consider the contents of a summary report to be appropriate supporting data within the 
116
meaning of 21 CFR 807.87(f) or (g); therefore, we recommend that you include a summary 
117
report.  The summary report should describe how this guidance document was used during 
118
the device development and testing and the methods or tests used.  The report should also 
119
include a summary of the test data or description of the acceptance criteria applied to address 
120
the risks identified in this document, as well as any additional risks specific to your device.  
121
This section provides suggestions about how to compose your Abbreviated 510(k) 
122
submission, including a suggested order and headings. 
123
 
124
1. 
Coversheet 
125
The coversheet should prominently identify the submission as an Abbreviated 510(k) 
126
and cite the title of this guidance document. 
127
128
2. 
Proposed labeling 
129
Include proposed labels, labeling, and advertisements sufficient to describe the 
130
device, its intended use, and the directions for its use.  21 CFR 807.87(e).  Refer to 
131
the section titled “Labeling” for specific information that you should include in the 
132
labeling for this type of device. 
133
 
134
3. 
Summary report 
135
The special controls for an Early Growth Response 1 (EGR1) Gene Fluorescence in-
136
situ Hybridization (FISH) Test System for Specimen Characterization have been 
137
outlined in regulation 21 CFR 864.1870.  The Abbreviated 510(k) submission should 
138
include a summary report in tabular format that contains the information required by 
139
these special controls as well as confirmation that the studies have been conducted 
140
and met the appropriate pre-specified acceptance criteria.  These studies are listed 
141
below with additional explanatory information intended to provide clarity about the 
142
content of the submission.

Device Information 
145
146
a. 
Device Intended Use/Indications for Use statement:  You should provide a Device 
147
Intended Use/Indications for Use statement. 
148
 
149
b. 
Description of all probes included in the kit:  You must provide a detailed 
150
description of all probes included in the kit.  21 CFR 864.1870(b)(1)(i).  The 
151
information you provide should include, but not necessarily be limited to, the 
152
identification of labeling moiety, the chromosome specificity (e.g., chromosome, 
153
band), and the nature of probes (e.g., break-apart, dual color, dual fusion).  
154
  
155
c. 
Purpose of Each Probe:  You must provide the purpose of each probe.  21 CFR 
156
864.1870(b)(1)(ii).  You should indicate the chromosomal abnormality the probe 
157
was designed to detect (e.g., deletion/amplification).  You should use standard 
158
scientific nomenclature and provide a glossary of terms where relevant. 
159
 
160
d. 
Probe Molecular Specificity:  You must provide probe molecular specificity.  21 
161
CFR 864.1870(b)(1)(iii).  You should provide end-sequencing information for each 
162
probe and its link to the reference human genome sequence. 
163
 
164
e. 
Probe Specificity:  You must provide probe specificity.  21 CFR 
165
864.1870(b)(1)(iv). You should provide evidence of specific binding to the 
166
expected chromosomal site (chromosome and band) for 5 or more metaphase 
167
samples from different normal individuals (indicate sex of each sample) and for 20 
168
or more consecutive intact metaphases for each sample.  When more than one 
169
site/technologist is used, each should perform a complete analysis of the 5 samples 
170
and 20 or more consecutive intact metaphase stated above.  You should list all 
171
results and annotate number and location of unexpected signals.  Provide specificity 
172
calculations with 95% confidence intervals.  
173
174
 
f. 
Probe Limits:  You must provide probe limits.  21 CFR 864.1870(b)(1)(v).  You 
175
should indicate the highest and lowest probe concentrations that produce acceptable 
176
signals.   
177
178
 
g. 
Probe Sensitivity:  You must provide probe sensitivity.  21 CFR 864.1870(b)(1)(vi).  
179
You should provide expected and observed signal count for 25 samples from 
180
different normal individuals (indicate sex), counting 200 consecutive scoreable 
181
nuclei from each sample.  You should provide sensitivity calculations with 95% 
182
confidence intervals.  You should describe expected values (cut-off) and provide 
183
calculations. 
184
185
 
h. 
Reagents:  You must provide a specification of the device’s required ancillary 
186
reagents, instrumentation and equipment.  21 CFR 864.1870(b)(1)(vii).

273

274
data identified in 21 CFR 864.1870(b)(1)(xiii)-(xviii) and a description of the studies

284
predicting therapeutic response or disease screening.”
