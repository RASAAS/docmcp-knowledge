# Renewal registration dossier

**Source:** NMPA Announcement No. 121 of 2021, Annex 6  
**Electronic directories:** domestic renewal dossier; imported renewal dossier

Section titles follow those electronic directories. The text under each section is a close translation of the official Chinese wording in Annex 6. Where a directory states the same point differently, that wording is included. A folder that has a name and no further official text is listed by name only.

Both directories mark the whole of Chapter 2 Overview, Chapter 5 Instructions for use and labelling specimens, Chapter 6A Quality management system documents, and Chapter 6B Quality management system information for the device under submission as not applicable. Those chapters have no further requirement.

## Chapter 1 Regulatory information

### CH1.2 Table of contents

The submitted dossier shall include a table of contents, covering every heading and subheading of this chapter, and shall state the contents number, the contents title, applicability, the uploaded file name, and the page numbers of the uploaded file, and shall give the page number of each item. Applicability shall state whether a CR folder applies.

Annex 6: the table of contents shall include every heading and subheading of this chapter and the page number of each item.

### CH1.4 Application form

Complete the form as required and upload the application form that carries the data-check code.

Annex 6: complete the form as required.

### CH1.6 Quality management system, full quality system, or other evidence

Domestic directory: a copy of the business licence or of the legal-person certificate of a public institution.

Imported directory: an overseas registrant shall submit the enterprise-qualification document.

Annex 6: a domestic registrant shall submit a copy of the business licence or of the legal-person certificate of a public institution; an overseas registrant shall submit the enterprise-qualification document.

### CH1.7 Certificate of free sale / marketing authorisation

Both directories have only this folder name and no further requirement.

Annex 6: for renewal of an imported medical device, the document of the country (region) where the registrant is registered or where the device is manufactured, approving the product for marketing, is not required.

### CH1.9 Pre-submission contacts and previous communications with the regulatory authority

1. During the validity of the registration certificate, if the registrant has communicated with the regulatory authority about the device in the form of a meeting, provide the following, as applicable:
   1. List the communications to which the regulatory authority replied.
   2. Questions the registrant expressly raised in the communication, and the advice given by the regulatory authority.
   3. State how those issues are addressed in this submission.
2. If this does not apply, expressly declare that, during the validity of the registration certificate, the device has no previous submission and/or pre-submission communication.

### CH1.11 Statements / certification / declaration

This level has no content of its own. Documents are filed under the headings below.

CH1.11.1: the registrant declares that the product under renewal meets the current national standards and industry standards, and provides the list of standards met.

CH1.11.5: the domestic directory reads “the registrant’s declaration that the materials submitted are true”. The imported directory reads “for an imported product, a declaration issued separately by the registrant and the agent that the materials submitted are true”.

CH1.11.7:

1. The product under renewal meets the requirements of the Measures for the Administration of Medical Device Registration and Filing and of the related regulations.
2. The product under renewal meets the classification requirements of the Rules for Classification of Medical Devices.

The declaration of conformity in Annex 6 also includes: the product under renewal has not changed. If the product has changed in a matter other than those stated in the registration certificate, it shall be stated that “the changes that have occurred are controlled through the quality management system, and the matters stated in the registration certificate have not changed”. The directories place that sentence under CH1.14. The list of current standards and the declaration of truthfulness are placed under CH1.11.1 and CH1.11.5.

### CH1.13 Letter of authorisation for the agent

The domestic directory has only this folder name and no further requirement.

Imported directory: the letter appointing the agent designated in China, the agent’s letter of commitment, and a copy of the agent’s business licence.

Annex 6: an overseas registrant shall submit the letter appointing the agent designated in China, the agent’s letter of commitment, and a copy of the agent’s business licence.

### CH1.14 Other regulatory information

1. Submit a copy of the original medical-device registration certificate and its annexes, and copies of each previous change-registration (filing) document and its annexes.
2. If, during the validity of the medical-device registration certificate, a new mandatory medical-device standard has been issued and has taken effect, and the change made so that the registered product meets the new mandatory standard is one for which change registration shall be carried out, the registrant shall submit copies of the change-registration (filing) document and its annexes already approved by the original approving authority before the renewal application. If the change made so that the registered product meets the new mandatory standard is one for which no change-registration procedure is required, or if the product meets the new mandatory standard without change, the registrant shall provide an explanation and the related evidence.
3. The registrant declares that the product under renewal has not changed. If the product has changed in a matter other than those stated in the registration certificate, it shall be stated that “the changes that have occurred are controlled through the quality management system, and the matters stated in the registration certificate have not changed”.

The following folders have only a name in both directories and no further requirement: CH1.1 Cover letter, CH1.3 List of terms/abbreviations, CH1.5 Product list, CH1.8 User fees, CH1.10 Acceptance review checklist, CH1.11.2 Environmental assessment, CH1.11.3 Clinical trial approval documents, CH1.11.4, CH1.11.6, CH1.12 Master file letter of authorisation.

## Chapter 3 Non-clinical data

### CH3.1 Table of contents

The table of contents of the materials submitted for this chapter, including the contents number, the contents title, applicability, the uploaded file name, and the page numbers of the uploaded file. Applicability shall state whether a CR folder applies.

Annex 6: the table of contents shall include every heading and subheading of this chapter and the page number of each item.

### CH3.4.2 Declaration of conformity and/or certification

Product technical requirement: if, during the validity of the original medical-device registration certificate, a change involving the product technical requirement occurred, the product technical requirement as amended according to the change-registration (filing) document shall be submitted.

CH3.4 has no content of its own; documents are filed under the headings below. The electronic directory has no CH3.4.3. CH3.4.1 List of standards has only the name.

### CH3.5 Non-clinical studies

This level has no content. No materials need be submitted.

### CH3.8 Other information

Where the original medical-device registration certificate states matters that are required to be completed, and those matters involve non-clinical studies, the related summary report shall be provided, with the corresponding materials attached.

CH3.2 Risk management, CH3.3 Essential principles of safety and performance checklist, CH3.6 Non-clinical study literature, CH3.7 Shelf life and packaging validation and its children, and the study-template folders, have only names in both directories and no further requirement.

## Chapter 4 Clinical evaluation

### CH4.2.1 Clinical evaluation data

Where the original medical-device registration certificate states matters that are required to be completed, and those matters involve clinical evaluation, the related summary report shall be provided, with the corresponding materials attached.

The table-of-contents wording of CH4.1 is the same as that of CH3.1. CH4.2, CH4.2.2, CH4.2.3, CH4.3, CH4.4, and the trial and study template folders have only names in both directories and no further requirement. CH4.5 Other clinical evidence has only the name.
