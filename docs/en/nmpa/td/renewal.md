# NMPA Renewal Registration Dossier

> Source: Notice **2021-121** Attachment 6.  
> Chinese: [延续注册](/zh/nmpa/td/renewal).

Prerequisite: **no certificate-listed changes** (or changes controlled under QMS without altering listed particulars). Substantive changes require change registration/filing first.

## Lean structure

1. Regulatory information (prior certificate pack, declarations of no change, standards compliance notes)  
2. Non-clinical — updated PTR only if PTR changed during validity; leftover non-clinical commitments  
3. Clinical — only leftover clinical commitments on the certificate  

Imported devices: home-country marketing authorization is **not** required for renewal.
