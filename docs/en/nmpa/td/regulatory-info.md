# Chapter 1: Regulatory information

**Source:** NMPA Announcement No. 121 of 2021, Annex 5, Chapter 1  
**Electronic directories:** domestic Class III registration dossier; imported Class II and Class III registration dossier

Section titles follow those electronic directories. The text under each section is a close translation of the official Chinese wording in Annex 5. Where a directory states the same point differently, that wording is included. A folder that has a name and no further official text is listed by name only.

Note in the imported Class II and Class III directory: the applicant for an imported product ticks whether original-language materials that are not in simplified Chinese characters are submitted. If the materials are in simplified Chinese characters, select “No”; each heading then has one upload channel, for the Chinese materials. If the materials are not in simplified Chinese characters, select “Yes”; each heading then shows two upload channels, Chinese materials and original-language materials. The applicability stated in this directory is the applicability of the Chinese materials. For the original-language materials, every heading has the same applicability as the Chinese materials except CH1.4, which is CR.

## CH1.1 Cover letter

The domestic Class III directory and the imported Class II and Class III directory have only this folder name and no further requirement.

## CH1.2 Table of contents

The submitted dossier shall include a table of contents, covering every heading and subheading of this chapter, and shall state the contents number, the contents title, applicability, the uploaded file name, and the page numbers of the uploaded file, and shall give the page number of each item. Applicability shall state whether a CR folder applies.

Annex 5: the table of contents shall include every heading and subheading of this chapter and the page number of each item.

## CH1.3 List of terms and abbreviations

Where applicable, terms or abbreviations that appear in the registration dossier and whose meaning needs to be made clear shall be defined according to the dossier as submitted.

## CH1.4 Application form

Complete the form as required and upload the application form that carries the data-check code. Upload the Application Form for Priority Review of Medical Devices, if any.

The domestic Class III directory also states: upload the Fee Preference Application Form for Small and Micro Enterprises, if any.

Annex 5: complete the form as required.

## CH1.5 Product list

In a table, list the models, specifications, structure and composition, and accessories of the products to be submitted, and, for each model and specification, the identifier (such as the number of the model or component, or the unique device identifier) and a description (such as dimensions and materials).

## CH1.6 Quality management system, full quality system, or other evidence

Domestic Class III directory:

1. A copy of the business licence or of the legal-person certificate of a public institution.
2. For a domestic device reviewed under the Special Review Procedure for Innovative Medical Devices, submit the statement that the innovation review has been passed.
3. For a device reviewed under the Emergency Review Procedure for Medical Devices, submit the statement that the emergency review has been passed.
4. Where manufacture is entrusted to another enterprise, provide the qualification documents of the entrusted enterprise (a copy of the business licence), the entrustment contract, and the quality agreement.
5. Where the registrant of an imported device applies for registration through a foreign-invested enterprise it has established in China, under the provisions on manufacture in China of imported devices, submit a statement or authorisation from the imported-device registrant consenting to the registration submission; also provide a document explaining the relationship (including legal liability) between the applicant and the imported-device registrant, with the relevant agreement, quality responsibility, and evidence of equity.

Imported Class II and Class III directory:

1. Evidence of enterprise qualification: a document issued by the company-registration authority or the medical-device authority of the country (region) where the overseas applicant is registered, showing that the overseas applicant is in existence and holds the corresponding qualification to manufacture medical devices; or a document issued by a third-party certification body showing that the overseas applicant holds the corresponding qualification to manufacture medical devices.
2. For an imported device reviewed under the Special Review Procedure for Innovative Medical Devices, submit the statement that the innovation review has been passed.
3. For an imported device reviewed under the Emergency Review Procedure for Medical Devices, submit the statement that the emergency review has been passed.
4. Where manufacture is entrusted to another enterprise, provide the qualification documents of the entrusted enterprise, the entrustment contract, and the quality agreement.

## CH1.7 Certificate of free sale / marketing authorisation

The domestic Class III directory has only this folder name and no further requirement.

Imported Class II and Class III directory:

1. A document issued by the medical-device authority of the country (region) where the overseas applicant is registered or where the device is manufactured, authorising the device to be marketed. An innovative device that is not marketed in the country (region) of registration or manufacture need not be submitted.
2. Where the country (region) of registration or manufacture does not regulate the product as a medical device, the applicant shall provide the relevant documents, including a document of the country (region) of registration or manufacture authorising the product to be marketed. An innovative device that is not marketed in the country (region) of registration or manufacture need not be submitted.

## CH1.8 User fees

The domestic Class III directory and the imported Class II and Class III directory have only this folder name and no further requirement.

## CH1.9 Pre-submission contacts and previous communications with the regulatory authority

1. Before submission, if the applicant has communicated with the regulatory authority about the device in the form of a meeting, or if the device is related to a previous registration submission, provide the following, as applicable:
   1. List the pre-submission communications to which the regulatory authority replied.
   2. The acceptance number of the previously submitted product.
   3. Materials of the previous pre-submission communication, such as information submitted before the meeting, the agenda, presentation slides, the final minutes, replies to action items from the meeting, and all application-related email.
   4. Issues the regulatory authority has already made clear in a previous submission (such as a withdrawn application or an application that was not granted marketing authorisation, or a clinical-trial approval application).
   5. Questions the applicant expressly raised in the pre-submission communication, and the advice given by the regulatory authority.
   6. State how those issues are addressed in this submission.
2. If this does not apply, expressly declare that the device has no previous submission and/or pre-submission communication.

## CH1.10 Acceptance review checklist

The domestic Class III directory and the imported Class II and Class III directory have only this folder name and no further requirement.

## CH1.11 Statements / certification / declaration

This level has no content of its own. Documents are filed under the headings below.

### CH1.11.1 List of standards and declaration of conformity

The applicant declares that the device meets the current national standards and industry standards, and provides the list of standards met.

### CH1.11.2 Environmental assessment

The domestic Class III directory and the imported Class II and Class III directory have only this folder name and no further requirement.

### CH1.11.3 Clinical trial approval documents

Where applicable, submit the clinical-trial approval opinion form for the medical device.

### CH1.11.4 Statement of indications that includes a prescription (Rx) or non-prescription (OTC) statement

The domestic Class III directory and the imported Class II and Class III directory have only this folder name and no further requirement.

### CH1.11.5 Declaration of truthfulness and accuracy

Domestic Class III directory: the applicant’s declaration that the materials submitted are true.

Imported Class II and Class III directory: the declaration of the applicant and the agent that the materials submitted are true.

### CH1.11.6 Summary and related qualification documents for a US FDA Class III device

The domestic Class III directory and the imported Class II and Class III directory have only this folder name and no further requirement.

### CH1.11.7 Declaration of conformity

The applicant declares:

1. The device meets the requirements of the Measures for the Administration of Medical Device Registration and Filing and of the related regulations.
2. The device meets the classification requirements of the Rules for Classification of Medical Devices.

Annex 5 also places the following two items in the same declaration as the two items above: the device meets the current national standards and industry standards, and a list of the standards met is provided; the materials submitted are true (issued by the applicant for a domestic product, and by the applicant and the agent separately for an imported product). The electronic directories place those two items under CH1.11.1 and CH1.11.5.

## CH1.12 Master file letter of authorisation

Where applicable, the applicant shall explain the citation of a master file. The applicant shall submit a letter of authorisation, issued by the owner of the master file or by its filing agent, authorising the applicant to cite information in the master file. The letter shall include the applicant citing the master file, the product name, the filed master-file number, and the pages or sections of the master file that are authorised to be cited.

## CH1.13 Letter of authorisation for the agent

The domestic Class III directory has only this folder name and no further requirement.

Imported Class II and Class III directory: the letter appointing the agent designated in China, the agent’s letter of commitment, and a copy of the agent’s business licence.

## CH1.14 Other regulatory information

Domestic Class III directory:

1. An enterprise applying for a priority product may upload the reasons and basis for priority review, if applicable.
2. A small or micro enterprise making a first registration application for an innovative device may upload the materials identifying it as a small or micro enterprise (the enterprise income-tax return for the previous year, which must bear the seal of the tax authority, or a valid statistical return for the previous year issued by the statistics authority), if applicable.

Imported Class II and Class III directory: an enterprise applying for a priority product may upload the reasons and basis for priority review, if applicable.
