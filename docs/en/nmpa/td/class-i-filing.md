# Class I medical device filing materials

**Source:** Requirements and Explanations for Class I Medical Device Filing Materials (NMPA Announcement No. 62 of 2022)

The national electronic submission directories have no Class I filing RPS directory corresponding to Class II and Class III registration. The headings and the text below follow the wording of that announcement.

## I. Filing materials

### (1) Class I medical device filing form

### (2) Associated documents

1. A domestic filer provides: a copy of the business licence or of the legal-person certificate of a public institution.

   Where manufacture is entrusted to another enterprise, the qualification documents of the entrusted enterprise (a copy of the business licence), a copy of the entrustment contract, and a copy of the quality agreement shall be provided.

2. An overseas filer provides:

   (1) Evidence of the overseas filer’s enterprise qualification: a document issued by the company-registration authority or the medical-device authority of the country (region) where the overseas filer is registered, showing that the overseas filer is in existence and holds the corresponding qualification to manufacture medical devices; or a document issued by a third-party certification body showing that the overseas filer holds the corresponding qualification to manufacture medical devices.

   (2) A document issued by the medical-device authority of the country (region) where the overseas filer is registered or where the device is manufactured, authorising the product to be marketed. Where the country (region) of registration or manufacture does not regulate the product as a medical device, the filer shall provide the relevant documents, including a document of the country (region) of registration or manufacture authorising the product to be marketed. An innovative device that is not marketed in the country (region) where the overseas filer is registered or where the device is manufactured need not be submitted.

   (3) The letter by which the overseas filer appoints an agent in China, the agent’s letter of commitment, and a copy of the agent’s business licence.

### (3) Product technical requirement

The product technical requirement shall be prepared in accordance with the Guideline for Writing Medical Device Product Technical Requirements. It mainly includes the functional and safety specifications of the finished device that can be judged objectively, and the test methods.

### (4) Product test report

The product test report shall be a full-performance self-test report or an entrusted test report. The product tested shall be representative.

Photographs of the physical product shall follow the test report. The photographs shall include the sample after all inner and outer packaging has been removed, and photographs of the inner and outer packaging as supplied. Where there are several models or specifications, provide photographs of a representative product.

### (5) Specimens of the instructions for use and of the label for the smallest sales unit

The instructions for use and the label shall meet the Provisions on the Administration of Medical Device Instructions for Use and Labelling, the Guideline for Writing Instructions for Use of In Vitro Diagnostic Reagents, and related requirements. The product performance in the instructions for use shall be consistent with the corresponding content of the product technical requirement. For an imported product, the original instructions for use approved or recognised by the overseas government authority, and a Chinese translation, shall be submitted.

### (6) Manufacturing information

An overview of the production process. For a non-active device, the manufacturing process shall be stated and the critical processes noted. For an active device, a descriptive account of the production process shall be provided, which may be a flow chart or an overview of the process. For an in vitro diagnostic reagent, the principal production process shall be outlined, including a description of the solid-phase carrier, the colour-development system, and similar items, and the basis on which they were determined; and the reaction system, including sample collection and handling, sample requirements, sample volume, reagent volume, reaction conditions, the calibration method (if needed), and the quality-control method.

Where there is more than one development or manufacturing site, the actual situation of each site shall be outlined.

Where manufacture is entrusted to another enterprise, the name, domicile, and manufacturing address of the entrusted enterprise shall be listed.

### (7) Declaration of conformity

1. A declaration that the Class I medical device filing requirements are met.
2. A declaration that the product meets the relevant classification requirements and the basis for that, including the relevant content of the Class I Medical Device Product Catalogue or the Classification Sub-catalogue for In Vitro Diagnostic Reagents. The exact classification basis shall be stated, and the sub-catalogue and the first-level and second-level product categories shall be identified.
3. A declaration that the product meets the current national standards and industry standards, together with the list of standards met.
4. A declaration that the filing materials submitted are true (issued by the filer for a domestic product, and by the filer and the agent separately for an imported product).

## II. Change-filing materials

### (1) Explanation of the change and the related associated documents

The explanation of the change shall be accompanied by a comparison table of the changed content of the filing-information form.

Where the product technical requirement changes, a comparison table of the changed content of the product technical requirement, and a product test report (if involved), shall be provided.

### (2) Associated documents

If the change involves a change to the associated documents of the filing, the new associated documents shall be submitted.

An overseas filer shall also submit the letter appointing the agent designated in China, the agent’s letter of commitment, and a copy of the agent’s business licence.

### (3) Declaration of conformity

1. A declaration that the Class I medical device filing requirements are met.
2. A declaration that the product meets the relevant classification requirements and the basis for that, including the relevant content of the Class I Medical Device Product Catalogue or the Classification Sub-catalogue for In Vitro Diagnostic Reagents. The exact classification basis shall be stated, and the sub-catalogue and the first-level and second-level product categories shall be identified.
3. A declaration that the product meets the current national standards and industry standards, together with the list of standards met.
4. A declaration that the filing materials submitted are true (issued by the filer for a domestic product, and by the filer and the agent separately for an imported product).

## III. Content requirements for the filing materials

### (1) Product name (product classification name)

The product name (excluding in vitro diagnostic reagents) shall in principle use directly a name listed under “examples of product names” in the Class I Medical Device Product Catalogue. Where there is a genuine need to adjust the product name for filing, a name that meets the Rules for Generic Naming of Medical Devices (CFDA Order No. 19) and the related naming guidelines shall be used.

The product classification name (product name) of an in vitro diagnostic reagent shall use the product classification name (product name) in the Classification Sub-catalogue for In Vitro Diagnostic Reagents.

### (2) Product description and intended use

The “product description” and “intended use” shall meet the corresponding requirements of the Class I Medical Device Product Catalogue and the Classification Sub-catalogue for In Vitro Diagnostic Reagents (together, the catalogues), and shall not go beyond the scope of the “product description”, the “intended use”, and the related content in the catalogues.

Where the product description in the Class I Medical Device Product Catalogue uses “usually composed of……”, that content only gives a representative structure and composition. At filing, the structure and composition may be described according to the product as filed. The words “usually composed of……” shall not be used. The words “composed of……” shall be used, and the specific composition shall be stated. Where there is a limiting statement such as “single use”, “repeated use”, “non-active product”, “applied to intact skin”, “without a dose-control function”, or “supplied non-sterile”, the “product description” of the filed product shall make that clear.

### (3) Model / specification

The model or specification shall not contain content that expressly or impliedly treats a disease, exaggerates the intended use, or is otherwise misleading or deceptive.

### (4) Name and domicile of the filer

The filer’s name and domicile in the filing form of a domestic filer shall be consistent with the business licence or the legal-person certificate of the public institution. The filer’s name, domicile, and manufacturing address in the filing form of an overseas filer shall be consistent with the overseas associated documents.

### Letter of appointment and letter of commitment of the agent designated by an overseas filer

The content of the appointment and of the commitment in the letter by which an overseas filer designates an agent, and in the agent’s letter of commitment, shall be consistent with the content of the filing.

### (6) Instructions for use

Content in the instructions for use that describes the product (including, but not limited to, structure and composition, principal constituents, intended use, method of use, precautions, and other parts that describe the product) shall not go beyond the corresponding content of the other filing materials.

If, before use, the user institution sterilises or disinfects the product according to the instructions for use, the instructions for use submitted at filing shall provide a sterilisation or disinfection method that has been validated.

## IV. Formal requirements for the filing materials

(1) The filing materials are complete. The filing form is filled in completely.

(2) Every document other than the associated documents shall be provided in Chinese. If an associated document is in a foreign language, a Chinese translation shall also be provided and shall be signed and sealed by the agent. Materials translated from foreign-language materials shall be submitted together with the original.

(3) Unless otherwise stated, the filing materials for a domestic product shall be signed and sealed by the filer. “Signed and sealed” means: the filer’s official seal, or the signature of its legal representative or responsible person together with the filer’s official seal.

(4) Unless otherwise stated, the original-language materials for an imported product shall be signed and sealed by the filer, and the Chinese materials shall be signed and sealed by the agent. “Signed and sealed” for the original-language materials means: the signature of the legal representative or responsible person of the filer, or that signature together with the seal of the organisation. “Signed and sealed” for the Chinese materials means: the agent’s official seal, or the signature of its legal representative or responsible person together with the agent’s official seal.

(5) Among the filing materials for an imported product, the associated documents, the declaration of conformity, and the instructions for use and labels provided by the overseas filer shall be submitted with a notarial certificate issued by a notary in the place where the filer is located. The notarial certificate may be obtained by electronic notarisation, but an explanation of the notarisation method, issued by the overseas filer, shall be submitted at the same time.

(6) Where the filer submits paper filing materials, the materials shall include a table of contents of what is submitted, including the first-level and second-level headings, and shall state the page number of each item in a table.

## V. Other requirements

(1) Products in the following two situations shall be filed as set out below.

1. For a Class I medical device supplied non-sterile, which the user institution must sterilise or disinfect according to the instructions for use before use in order to meet the clinical need (for example, a reusable ophthalmic scalpel supplied non-sterile), the “product description” shall state “supplied non-sterile; sterilised by the user institution according to the instructions for use before use” or “supplied non-sterile; disinfected by the user institution according to the instructions for use before use”. The “instructions for use” shall provide the sterilisation or disinfection method. The sterilisation or disinfection method provided, and the number of sterilisation or disinfection cycles the product can withstand, shall have been confirmed.

2. For a Class I medical device supplied non-sterile, which is not sterilised or disinfected again before use, but which itself has a microbial-limit requirement in order to meet the clinical need (for example, a wound dressing managed as a Class I medical device that contacts a wound), the “product description” shall state the microbial limit that must be met. The “product technical requirement” shall state the microbial limit that must be met. The “manufacturing information” shall state what measures the manufacturer has taken so that the product meets the specified microbial limit, and which standard’s microbial-limit requirement is currently met (if applicable).

(2) The four product types “09-02-03 physical cooling devices”, “09-03-08 accessories for light-therapy equipment”, “14-10-02 wound dressings”, and “20-03-11 acupoint pressure-stimulation devices” in the Class I Medical Device Product Catalogue shall not contain traditional Chinese medicine, chemical drugs, biological products, disinfectant or antibacterial ingredients, natural plants or their extracts, or other ingredients that exert a pharmacological, immunological, or metabolic action, or ingredients that can be absorbed by the human body, including but not limited to the ingredients listed in the appendix to the Class I Medical Device Product Catalogue.

When “09-02-03 physical cooling devices” in the Class I Medical Device Product Catalogue is filed, the product name shall use directly a name listed under “examples of product names” in the catalogue, and the intended use shall not go beyond the intended use specified in the catalogue.

(3) Combination packs. A product formed by combining more than one medical device that must be used together to achieve a given intended use may be filed as a Class I medical device if every product in the combination is a Class I medical device (and the combination contains no non-medical-device product in any form) and the combination does not change the intended use of each constituent device. The product name shall reflect the character of the combination and shall in principle be named according to the principal clinical intended use. The content of the name shall stay within the “product category (first or second level)” of the catalogue to which it belongs and within the “intended use” of each product included, such as an upper-limb internal-fixation surgical instrument (pack) or a knee-joint surgical instrument (pack). The “product description” shall include every constituent medical device and shall state the “product description” and “intended use” of each, and the basic content shall be consistent with the corresponding content of the catalogue. The classification code of a combination pack is the classification code of the medical device in the pack that plays the principal role in its intended use.
