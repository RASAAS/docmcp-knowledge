# Change registration dossier

**Source:** NMPA Announcement No. 121 of 2021, Annex 7, Requirements and explanations for change-registration materials  
**Electronic directories:** domestic change-registration dossier; imported change-registration dossier

Section titles follow those electronic directories. The text under each section is a close translation of the official Chinese wording in Annex 7. Where a directory states the same point differently, that wording is included. A folder that has a name and no further official text is listed by name only. The electronic directories have no CH3.4.3.

## Chapter 1 Regulatory information

### CH1.2 Table of contents

The submitted dossier shall include a table of contents, covering every heading and subheading of this chapter, and shall state the contents number, the contents title, applicability, the uploaded file name, and the page numbers of the uploaded file, and shall give the page number of each item. Applicability shall state whether a CR folder applies.

Annex 7: the table of contents shall include every heading and subheading of this chapter and the page number of each item.

### CH1.4 Application form

Complete the form as required and upload the application form that carries the data-check code.

Annex 7: complete the form as required.

### CH1.6 Quality management system, full quality system, or other evidence

Domestic directory: a copy of the business licence or of the legal-person certificate of a public institution.

The imported directory follows Annex 7 on the enterprise-qualification document the overseas registrant shall submit: according to whether the change requires a new enterprise-qualification document in the country (region) where the overseas registrant is registered or where the device is manufactured, submit the corresponding enterprise-qualification document.

### CH1.7 Certificate of free sale / marketing authorisation

The domestic directory has only this folder name and no further requirement.

Imported directory: if the change, in the country (region) where the overseas registrant is registered or where the device is manufactured, requires a new document from the medical-device authority authorising the product to be marketed, that document shall be submitted. If the change does not require approval by the medical-device authority of the country (region) of registration or of the place of manufacture, that shall be explained. An innovative device that is not marketed abroad need not be submitted.

### CH1.9 Pre-submission contacts and previous communications with the regulatory authority

1. Before this change application is submitted, if the registrant has communicated with the regulatory authority about the device in the form of a meeting, provide the following, as applicable:
   1. List the communications to which the regulatory authority replied.
   2. Questions the registrant expressly raised in the communication, and the advice given by the regulatory authority.
   3. State how those issues are addressed in this submission.
2. If this does not apply, expressly declare that the device has no previous submission and/or pre-submission communication.

### CH1.11 Statements / certification / declaration

This level has no content of its own. Documents are filed under the headings below.

CH1.11.1 List of standards and declaration of conformity: the registrant declares that the device meets the current national standards and industry standards, and provides the list of standards met.

CH1.11.5 Declaration of truthfulness and accuracy: the domestic directory reads “the registrant’s declaration that the materials submitted are true”. The imported directory reads “a declaration, issued separately by the registrant and the agent, that the materials submitted are true”.

CH1.11.7 Declaration of conformity:

1. The device meets the requirements of the Measures for the Administration of Medical Device Registration and Filing and of the related regulations.
2. The device meets the classification requirements of the Rules for Classification of Medical Devices.

Annex 7 also places, in the same declaration as the two items above, conformity to the current national standards and industry standards together with the list of standards met, and the guarantee that the materials submitted are true (issued by the registrant for a domestic product, and by the registrant and the agent separately for an imported product). The electronic directories place those two items under CH1.11.1 and CH1.11.5.

### CH1.13 Letter of authorisation for the agent

The domestic directory has only this folder name and no further requirement.

Imported directory: the letter appointing the agent designated in China, the agent’s letter of commitment, and a copy of the agent’s business licence.

### CH1.14 Other regulatory information

A copy of the original medical-device registration certificate and its annexes, and copies of each previous change-registration (filing) document and its annexes, shall be submitted.

The following folders have only a name in both directories and no further requirement: CH1.1 Cover letter, CH1.3 List of terms and abbreviations, CH1.5 Product list, CH1.8 User fees, CH1.10 Acceptance review checklist, CH1.11.2 Environmental assessment, CH1.11.3 Clinical trial approval documents, CH1.11.4 Statement of indications that includes a prescription (Rx) or non-prescription (OTC) statement, CH1.11.6 Summary and related qualification documents for a US FDA Class III device, CH1.12 Master file letter of authorisation.

## Chapter 2 Overview

### CH2.1 Table of contents

The submitted dossier shall include a table of contents, covering every heading and subheading of this chapter, and shall state the contents number, the contents title, applicability, the uploaded file name, and the page numbers of the uploaded file, and shall give the page number of each item. Applicability shall state whether a CR folder applies.

### CH2.2 Submission overview

Describe in detail the change, and the specific reason and purpose of the change.

Annex 7 titles this section “Overview”.

### CH2.4 Device description

This level has no content of its own. Documents are filed under the headings below.

### CH2.4.1 Comprehensive description of composition, function, and principle of operation

According to the specific change, provide the corresponding explanation and comparison table, including the following situations.

Domestic directory:

1. Change of product name.
2. Change of the product technical requirement.
3. Change of model or specification.
4. Change of structure and composition.
5. Change of scope of application.
6. Change of “other content” in the registration certificate.
7. Other changes.

The imported directory and Annex 7 add, after item 5: change of the manufacturing address of an imported medical device. After that come the change of “other content” in the registration certificate, and other changes.

The following folders have only a name in both directories and no further requirement: CH2.3 Pre-market application overview and certificates, CH2.4.2 Device packaging description, CH2.4.3 Device development history, CH2.4.4 Reference to and comparison with similar and/or previous-generation devices (marketed in China or abroad), CH2.4.5 Substantial equivalence discussion, CH2.5 and its children (intended use, expected use environment / installation requirements, information related to use in children, contraindications), CH2.6 and its children (global marketing status, global adverse events and recalls, global sales, adverse events and recall rates, evaluation / inspection reports), CH2.7 Other submission overview information.

## Chapter 3 Non-clinical data

### CH3.1 Table of contents

The submitted dossier shall include a table of contents, covering every heading and subheading of this chapter, and shall state the contents number, the contents title, applicability, the uploaded file name, and the page numbers of the uploaded file, and shall give the page number of each item. Applicability shall state whether a CR folder applies.

### CH3.2 Risk management

Product risk-management documentation related to the change.

The product risk-management documentation records the risk-management process and the results of its review. The following shall be provided, with traceability through each of the following processes for every hazard that has been determined.

1. Risk analysis: identification of the scope of the device and of characteristics related to safety, identification of hazards, and estimation of the risk of each hazardous situation.
2. Risk evaluation: for each identified hazardous situation, evaluate and decide whether the risk needs to be reduced and, if so, describe how the corresponding risk control is carried out.
3. Risk control: describe what is done to control risk in order to reduce it.
4. Judgment of the acceptability of any one or more residual risks.
5. An overall evaluation that the product risk is acceptable in comparison with the benefit.

The opening sentence of Annex 7 is: product risk-management documentation related to the change shall be submitted.

### CH3.3 Essential principles of safety and performance checklist

Both directories have only this folder name and no further requirement. The non-clinical materials for change registration in Annex 7 do not list this checklist separately.

### CH3.4 Standards

This level has no content of its own. Documents are filed under the headings below. The electronic directory has no CH3.4.3.

#### CH3.4.1 List of standards

Where applicable, the following shall be submitted.

The device under application shall meet the applicable mandatory standards. For a mandatory industry standard, if the structural characteristics, intended use, or manner of use of the device are not within the scope of that standard, the registrant shall state that the mandatory standard does not apply and shall provide verified supporting evidence.

#### CH3.4.2 Declaration of conformity and/or certification

Where applicable, the following shall be submitted.

1. Product technical requirement

   Where a mandatory medical-device standard has been revised, or another change has occurred, and the product technical requirement changes, the specific content of that change shall be stated.

2. Product test report

   A test report for the changed part of the product technical requirement may be submitted in either of the following forms:

   (1) A self-test report issued by the registrant.

   (2) A test report issued by a qualified medical-device testing institution to which the test was entrusted.

### CH3.5 Non-clinical studies

1. Analyse and explain the effect of the changed part on the safety and effectiveness of the product.
2. According to the change, provide a non-clinical study summary, describe each study that was carried out, and outline the methods and the conclusions.
3. According to the non-clinical study summary, provide the study materials under the corresponding heading below. Each set of study materials shall generally include the study protocol and the study report.

Item 2 of Annex 7 reads “provide the corresponding study materials; each set of study materials shall generally include the study protocol and the study report”, and does not have the words “under the corresponding heading below” of item 3.

Under CH3.5 the directories still list, separately, physical and mechanical performance, chemical/material characterisation, electrical systems, radiation safety, standalone software / software components, biocompatibility and toxicological evaluation, non-material-mediated pyrogenicity, biologically sourced materials, sterilisation validation, animal studies, and usability / human factors. Those folders, and “[study description, study number, start date] / summary / full report / statistical data”, have only names in both directories and no further requirement.

### CH3.8 Other information

Domestic directory: for a medical device exempt from clinical evaluation, if a change of the kind described above occurs and may affect the safety and effectiveness of the product and the demonstration that the device is equivalent to a product described in the Catalogue of Medical Devices Exempt from Clinical Evaluation, the applicant shall, in accordance with the Technical Guideline for the Comparison Description of Products Listed in the Catalogue of Medical Devices Exempt from Clinical Evaluation, demonstrate the safety and effectiveness of the product as to basic principle, structure and composition, performance, safety, and scope of application.

The imported directory and Annex 7 open that sentence as: for a Class II or Class III medical device exempt from clinical evaluation, if a change of the kind described above occurs……

CH3.6 Non-clinical study literature, and CH3.7 Shelf life and packaging validation and its children, have only names in both directories and no further requirement.

## Chapter 4 Clinical evaluation

Opening sentence of Annex 7 for this chapter: for a Class II or Class III medical device that requires clinical evaluation, if a change of the kind described above occurs and may affect the safety and effectiveness of the product, and clinical evaluation is involved, the applicable clinical evaluation data shall be provided as required.

### CH4.2.1 Clinical evaluation data

For a medical device that requires clinical evaluation, if a change of the kind described above occurs and may affect the safety and effectiveness of the product, and clinical evaluation is involved, the applicable clinical evaluation data shall be provided as required.

1. Product description and development background: basic information on the device, the scope of application, existing diagnostic or treatment methods and the clinical use of the medical devices involved, the relationship of the device to existing diagnostic or treatment methods, and the clinical effect expected to be achieved.
2. State the scope covered by the clinical evaluation. If part of the device may be exempt from clinical evaluation, describe its structure and composition and state the reason for the exemption.
3. Clinical evaluation pathway: according to the scope of application, technical characteristics, and existing clinical data, choose an appropriate pathway, including the equivalent-device pathway and/or the clinical-trial pathway.
4. If evaluation is by the equivalent-device pathway, submit comparison data between the device and the equivalent device as to scope of application, technical characteristics, and biological characteristics. Collect, assess, and analyse the clinical data of the equivalent device and form the clinical evidence. Where applicable, describe the differences and submit sufficient scientific evidence that the two have the same safety and effectiveness.

### CH4.2.2 Clinical trial data

If evaluation is by the clinical-trial pathway, submit the clinical trial protocol, the clinical trial report, and a sample informed-consent form, and attach the clinical trial database (the raw database, the analysis database, the explanatory documents, and the program code).

Annex 7 also includes, in the same sentence, the written opinion of the ethics committee of the clinical trial institution agreeing that the trial may be conducted. The domestic change-registration directory places that sentence under CH4.2.3, Clinical literature review and other related materials, and CH4.3 has only the folder name. The imported change-registration directory places that sentence under CH4.3, Ethics committee approval documents, and CH4.2.3 has only the folder name. The imported directory has no CH4.4. The domestic directory has CH4.4, Clinical trial sites and ethics committee contact information, with only the folder name.

### CH4.5 Other clinical evidence

Where applicable, provide the abstract, report, and data of the evaluation for the corresponding item.

The table-of-contents wording of CH4.1 is the same as CH4.1 of Chapter 4 for initial registration. CH4.2, the trial-description folder and its summary / report / data, and the study-description folder and its summary / full report / statistical data, have only names in both directories and no further requirement.

## Chapter 5 Instructions for use and labelling specimens

Annex 7 titles this chapter “Instructions for use”.

### CH5.3 Instructions for use

Where applicable, the changed content shall be explained in detail in a comparison table, and the instructions for use before the change and the instructions for use after the change shall be submitted. The content of the instructions for use shall meet the Provisions on the Administration of Medical Device Instructions for Use and Labelling and the requirements of the related laws, regulations, rules, normative documents, and mandatory standards.

If this does not apply, a corresponding explanation shall be provided.

### CH5.10 Other labelling materials

If the device has other documents that supplement the product information, then, where applicable, the changed content shall be explained in detail in a comparison table, and the documents before and after the change shall be submitted.

If this does not apply, a corresponding explanation shall be provided.

The table-of-contents wording of CH5.1 is the same as CH5.1 of Chapter 5 for initial registration. CH5.2 Product/package labels, CH5.4 Electronic instructions for use, CH5.5 Instructions for use for the physician, CH5.6 Instructions for use for the patient, CH5.7 Manual for technicians/operators, CH5.8 Patient file labels/cards and implant registration cards, and CH5.9 Product promotional materials have only names in both directories and no further requirement.

## Chapters 6A and 6B Quality management system documents

### CH6A.1 Application summary letter

1. Where a registered product undergoes a change-registration situation described above, the registrant shall undertake that, according to the specific change, the quality management system already established has been adjusted as required by the related regulations, and that a quality management system inspection may be accepted at any time.
2. Where the specific reason or purpose of the change put forward by the registrant involves a change of product design, raw materials, manufacturing process, scope of application, or method of use, the quality management system inspection shall cover the changed part. For the remaining changes, a quality management system inspection is generally not required.

Where a quality management system inspection is required, the registrant shall undertake that, as required by the related regulations and according to the specific change, the quality management system has been adjusted accordingly, and that a quality management system inspection may be accepted at any time. Describe in detail the quality management system changes for the items involved in the product change, and under the headings below submit the materials for each applicable item. Where an item does not apply, state the reason.

Annex 7 also states: where a quality management system inspection is required, the materials for this part shall be submitted as required by Annex 5.

The wording of the procedure folders below matches Chapter 6 of Annex 5. The titles follow the change-registration directories: CH6A.3.1 Product description information, CH6A.3.2 General manufacturing information, CH6A.4 Quality management system procedures, CH6A.5 Management responsibility procedures, CH6A.6 Resource management procedures, CH6A.7 Product realisation procedures and their design and development, purchasing, production and service control, and control of monitoring and measuring devices, CH6A.8 Measurement, analysis, and improvement procedures, CH6A.9 Other quality system procedures. CH6A.3 has no content of its own; documents are filed under the headings below. CH6A.3.3 Other forms has only the name.

### CH6B.2 Quality management system information

Annex 7 titles this section “Quality management system documents”. Under the quality management system procedures above, the registrant shall form the quality management system documents and records for the items involved in the product change:

1. The registrant’s basic-information form.
2. The registrant’s organisation chart.
3. The general layout of the manufacturing site and the layout of the production areas.
4. Where the production process has a cleanliness requirement, a copy of the environmental test report issued by a qualified testing institution (with the floor plan attached).
5. The production-process flow chart, which shall mark the principal control points and items and the source and quality-control method for the principal raw materials and purchased parts.
6. A list of the principal production equipment and inspection equipment (including the equipment for incoming inspection, in-process inspection, and final release inspection; where clean production is required, the environmental monitoring equipment shall also be provided).
7. The registrant’s quality management system self-inspection report.
8. Where applicable, a comparison of the product proposed for inspection with a product that has previously passed inspection, as to production conditions, production process, and similar matters.
