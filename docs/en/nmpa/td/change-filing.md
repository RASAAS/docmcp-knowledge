# Change filing materials

**Source:** NMPA Announcement No. 121 of 2021, Annex 7, Requirements and explanations for change-filing materials  
**Electronic directories:** domestic change-filing dossier; imported change-filing dossier

Section titles follow those electronic directories. The text under each section is a close translation of the official Chinese wording in Annex 7. Where a directory states the same point differently, that wording is included. A folder that has a name and no further official text is listed by name only.

Both directories mark the whole of Chapter 3 Non-clinical data, Chapter 4 Clinical evaluation, Chapter 5 Instructions for use and labelling specimens, Chapter 6A Quality management system documents, and Chapter 6B Quality management system information for the device under submission as not applicable. Those chapters have no further requirement.

## Chapter 1 Regulatory information

### CH1.2 Table of contents

The submitted dossier shall include a table of contents, stating the contents number, the contents title, applicability, the uploaded file name, and the page numbers of the uploaded file. Applicability shall state whether a CR folder applies.

Annex 7: the table of contents shall include every heading and subheading of this chapter and the page number of each item.

### CH1.4 Filing form

Complete the form as required and upload the filing form that carries the data-check code.

Annex 7: complete the form as required.

### CH1.6 Quality management system, full quality system, or other evidence

Domestic directory: a domestic registrant shall submit a copy of the business licence and a copy of the organisation-code certificate.

Annex 7: a domestic registrant shall submit a copy of the business licence or of the legal-person certificate of a public institution.

Imported directory: according to whether the change requires a new enterprise-qualification document in the country (region) where the overseas registrant is registered or where the device is manufactured, submit the corresponding enterprise-qualification document.

### CH1.7 Certificate of free sale / marketing authorisation

The domestic directory has only this folder name and no further requirement.

Imported directory: if the change, in the country (region) where the overseas registrant is registered or where the device is manufactured, requires a new document from the medical-device authority authorising the product to be marketed, that document shall be submitted. If the change does not require approval by the medical-device authority of the country (region) of registration or of the place of manufacture, that shall be explained. An innovative device that is not marketed abroad need not be submitted.

### CH1.11 Statements / certification / declaration

This level has no content of its own. Documents are filed under the headings below.

CH1.11.1: the registrant declares that the device meets the current national standards and industry standards, and provides the list of standards met.

CH1.11.5: the domestic directory reads “the registrant’s declaration that the materials submitted are true”. The imported directory reads “a declaration, issued separately by the registrant and the agent, that the materials submitted are true”.

CH1.11.7:

1. The device meets the requirements of the Measures for the Administration of Medical Device Registration and Filing and of the related regulations.
2. The device meets the classification requirements of the Rules for Classification of Medical Devices.

Annex 7 also places, in the same place as the two items above, conformity to the current national standards and industry standards together with the list of standards met, and the guarantee that the materials submitted are true (issued by the registrant for a domestic product, and by the registrant and the agent separately for an imported product).

### CH1.13 Letter of authorisation for the agent

The domestic directory has only this folder name and no further requirement.

Imported directory: the letter appointing the agent designated in China, the agent’s letter of commitment, and a copy of the agent’s business licence.

### CH1.14 Other regulatory information

Submit a copy of the original medical-device registration certificate and its annexes, and copies of each previous change-registration (filing) document and its annexes.

The following folders have only a name in both directories and no further requirement: CH1.1 Cover letter, CH1.3 List of terms/abbreviations, CH1.5 Product list, CH1.8 User fees, CH1.9 Pre-submission contacts and previous communications with the regulatory authority, CH1.10 Acceptance review checklist, CH1.11.2 Environmental assessment, CH1.11.3, CH1.11.4, CH1.11.6, CH1.12 Master file letter of authorisation. In the domestic directory the name of CH1.11.3 is “Clinical trial certificate”. In the imported directory it is “Clinical trial approval documents”.

## Chapter 2 Overview

### CH2.1 Table of contents

The table of contents of the materials submitted for this chapter, including the contents number, the contents title, applicability, the uploaded file name, and the page numbers of the uploaded file. Applicability shall state whether a CR folder applies.

### CH2.2 Submission overview

Describe in detail the change, and the specific reason and purpose of the change.

The domestic directory places “the registrant’s declaration on the change” before that sentence. The imported directory reads “the registrant’s declaration on the change”. Annex 7 reads “the registrant’s explanation of the change”.

### CH2.4.1 Comprehensive description of composition, function, and principle of operation

Domestic directory: according to the specific change, provide the corresponding documents, including the following situations:

(1) Change of the registrant’s name: a detailed explanation of the change and the corresponding evidence.

(2) Change of the registrant’s domicile: a detailed explanation of the change and the corresponding evidence.

(3) Change of the manufacturing address of a domestic medical device: the manufacturing licence after the change shall be provided.

Imported directory:

(1) Change of the registrant’s name: the corresponding associated documents.

(2) Change of the registrant’s domicile: the corresponding associated documents.

(3) Change of agent: a letter from the registrant appointing the new agent, and a letter of commitment issued by the new agent; a copy of the new agent’s business licence.

(4) Change of the agent’s domicile: copies of the business licence before and after the change.

Annex 7 states, by situation:

1. Change of the registrant’s name: the approval notice of the enterprise-name change (domestic registrant) and/or the corresponding associated documents.
2. Change of the registrant’s domicile: copies of the business licence or of the legal-person certificate of a public institution before and after the change (domestic registrant) and/or the corresponding associated documents.
3. Change of the manufacturing address of a domestic medical device: the manufacturing licence after the change and its annexes.
4. Change of agent: a letter from the registrant appointing the new agent, and a letter of commitment issued by the new agent; a copy of the new agent’s business licence.
5. Change of the agent’s domicile: copies of the business licence before and after the change.

CH2.4 has no content of its own; documents are filed under the headings below. CH2.3, CH2.4.2 through CH2.4.5, CH2.5 and its children, CH2.6 and its children, and CH2.7 have only names in both directories and no further requirement.
