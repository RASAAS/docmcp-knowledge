# NMPA Change Filing Dossier

> Source: Notice **2021-121** Attachment 7 — change filing section.  
> Chinese: [变更备案](/zh/nmpa/td/change-filing).

For administrative changes (registrant name/address, domestic manufacturing site, agent). Much lighter than [change registration](/en/nmpa/td/change-registration): filing form, associated documents, change description, conformity declaration.
