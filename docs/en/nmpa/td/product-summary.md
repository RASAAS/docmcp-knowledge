# Chapter 2: Overview

**Source:** NMPA Announcement No. 121 of 2021, Annex 5, Chapter 2  
**Electronic directories:** domestic Class III registration dossier; imported Class II and Class III registration dossier

Section titles follow those electronic directories. The text under each section is a close translation of the official Chinese wording in Annex 5. Where a directory states the same point differently, that wording is included. A folder that has a name and no further official text is listed by name only.

## CH2.1 Table of contents

The submitted dossier shall include a table of contents, covering every heading and subheading of this chapter, and shall state the contents number, the contents title, applicability, the uploaded file name, and the page numbers of the uploaded file, and shall give the page number of each item. Applicability shall state whether a CR folder applies.

Annex 5: the table of contents shall include every heading and subheading of this chapter and the page number of each item.

## CH2.2 Submission overview

1. Describe the generic name of the device and the basis on which it was determined.
2. Describe the regulatory class of the device, including the name of the classification sub-catalogue, the first-level product category, the second-level product category, the regulatory class, and the classification code.
3. Describe the scope of application of the device.
4. Where applicable, describe background or particular detail of the device, such as a historical overview, information submitted on previous occasions, and the relationship to other products already approved for marketing.

## CH2.3 Pre-market application overview and certificates

The domestic Class III directory and the imported Class II and Class III directory have only this folder name and no further requirement.

## CH2.4 Device description

This level has no content of its own. Documents are filed under the headings below.

### CH2.4.1 Comprehensive description of composition, function, and principle of operation

1. Description of the device and its operating principle

   (1) Non-active medical devices

   Describe the working principle, the mechanism of action (if applicable), the structure and composition, the raw materials (the composition of materials in direct or indirect contact with the user and/or the patient; if the device contains a biological material or a derivative, describe the source and the raw material, the intended purpose, and the principal mode of action; if the device contains an active pharmaceutical ingredient or a drug, describe the drug name, the intended purpose, the principal mode of action, and the source), the state as supplied and the method of sterilisation (if applicable, describe who carries out sterilisation, the method, and the sterilisation shelf life), structural diagrams and/or product illustrations, the method of use and illustrations (if applicable), and features that distinguish it from other similar products.

   (2) Active medical devices

   Describe the working principle, the mechanism of action (if applicable), the structure and composition, the principal functions and the functions of the constituent parts (such as critical components and software), product illustrations (including identification, interfaces, the control panel, applied parts, and other detail), and features that distinguish it from other similar products. Where there are several constituent parts, state how they are connected or assembled.

2. Models and specifications

   Where the product has more than one model or specification, the differences shall be stated. A comparison table, or pictures or charts with explanatory text, shall describe the structure and composition (or configuration), functions, product features and operating modes, and technical parameters of each model and specification.

### CH2.4.2 Device packaging description

1. State the packaging information for every constituent of the product. For a sterile device, state the information on its sterile barrier system. For a device that has a microbial-limit requirement, state the packaging information that maintains that limit. State how the end user can clearly recognise that the packaging is intact.
2. If the user must package the device or an accessory before sterilisation, provide the information for correct packaging (such as materials, composition, and dimensions).

### CH2.4.3 Device development history

Set out the background and purpose of the development of the product under application. If a similar product or a previous-generation product was used as a reference, provide information on that product and state why it was chosen as the development reference.

### CH2.4.4 Reference to and comparison with similar and/or previous-generation devices (marketed in China or abroad)

In a table, compare the device under application with similar products and/or previous-generation products as to working principle, structure and composition, materials of manufacture, performance specifications, mode of action (such as implantation or intervention), and scope of application.

### CH2.4.5 Substantial equivalence discussion

The domestic Class III directory and the imported Class II and Class III directory have only this folder name and no further requirement.

## CH2.5 Indications and/or intended use, and contraindications

This level has no content of its own. Documents are filed under the headings below.

### CH2.5.1 Intended use; purpose of use; intended user; scope of application

1. Scope of application
   1. State the treatment or diagnostic function the device can provide. The medical process may be described (such as in vivo or in vitro diagnosis, rehabilitation, treatment monitoring, contraception, or disinfection). State the disease or condition the device is to diagnose, treat, prevent, mitigate, or cure, the parameters to be monitored, and other considerations related to the scope of application.
   2. The intended use of the device, and the stage of care to which it applies (such as post-treatment monitoring or rehabilitation).
   3. Identify the intended user and the skill, knowledge, or training required to operate or use the product.
   4. State whether the product is for single use or for repeated use.
   5. State the other products used in combination with it to achieve the intended use.
2. Population

   Information on the target patient population (such as adults, neonates, infants, or children), or a statement that no particular population is intended to be treated; information on patient-selection criteria; and parameters and factors to be considered during use.

### CH2.5.2 Expected use environment / installation requirements

1. The place where the product is intended to be used, such as a medical institution, a laboratory, an ambulance, or the home.
2. Environmental conditions that may affect its safety and effectiveness, such as temperature, humidity, pressure, movement, vibration, and altitude.

### CH2.5.3 Information related to use in children

If the target patient population includes neonates, infants, or children, describe the non-adult groups in which the device is intended to treat, diagnose, prevent, mitigate, or cure a disease or condition.

### CH2.5.4 Contraindications

Where applicable, if a risk/benefit assessment concludes that the product is not recommended for certain diseases, conditions, or specific populations (such as children, older people, pregnant or lactating women, or persons with hepatic or renal impairment), that shall be stated expressly.

## CH2.6 Global marketing history

This level has no content of its own. Documents are filed under the headings below.

### CH2.6.1 Global marketing status

As of the date the registration application is submitted, the dates of marketing approval and the sales of the device in each country or region. If the device differed when marketed in different countries or regions (such as design, labelling, or technical parameters), describe each difference.

The directories also state: if this does not apply, a related explanation shall be submitted.

### CH2.6.2 Global adverse events and recalls

Where applicable, in separate lists, describe adverse events and recalls after marketing, the time of each, and in each case the handling and the solution adopted by the applicant, including measures taken on the applicant’s own initiative to control product risk, reporting to the technical institution for medical-device adverse-event monitoring, and the investigation and handling by the relevant authority.

Also analyse and evaluate those adverse events and recalls, state why they occurred, and explain the effect on safety and effectiveness. If the numbers are large, summarise the number involved in each type of event.

The directories also state: if this does not apply, a related explanation shall be submitted.

### CH2.6.3 Global sales, adverse events, and recall rates

Where applicable, submit a summary of sales quantities of the device in each country (region) over the past five years, give the adverse-event rate and the recall rate in each country (region) as follows, and provide the key analysis of how the rates are calculated.

For example: adverse-event rate = number of adverse events ÷ sales quantity × 100%; recall rate = number of recalls ÷ sales quantity × 100%. A rate may be calculated per patient-year of use or per use. The applicant shall describe the method of calculation.

The directories also state: if this does not apply, a related explanation shall be submitted.

### CH2.6.4 Evaluation / inspection reports

The domestic Class III directory and the imported Class II and Class III directory have only this folder name and no further requirement.

## CH2.7 Other submission overview information

1. Where applicable, state in detail the other products used together with the device to achieve the intended use.
2. For a component or a cooperating accessory that has already been approved, provide the registration-certificate number and the registration-certificate information published on the official website of the NMPA.

The directories also state: if this does not apply, a related explanation shall be submitted.
