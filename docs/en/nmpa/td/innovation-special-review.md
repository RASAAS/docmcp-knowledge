# Innovation special review application materials

**Source:** Guide for Preparing Application Materials for the Special Review of Innovative Medical Devices (annex to NMPA Announcement No. 127 of 2018)  
**Electronic directory:** electronic directory for the special review of innovative medical devices

Section titles follow the numbers and names of that electronic directory. The text under each section is a close translation of the official Chinese wording of the guide. Where the directory states the same point differently, that wording is included. A folder that has a name and no further official text is listed by name only.

Opening of the guide: this guide is issued to standardise applications for the special review of innovative medical devices, improve the quality of application materials, and promote innovation in medical devices, in accordance with the Regulations on the Supervision and Administration of Medical Devices (State Council Order No. 680), the Measures for the Administration of Medical Device Registration (CFDA Order No. 4), the Measures for the Administration of Registration of In Vitro Diagnostic Reagents (CFDA Order No. 5), and the Special Review Procedure for Innovative Medical Devices (NMPA Announcement No. 83 of 2018). This guide standardises the preparation and writing of application materials for innovative medical devices, so that applicants know the points that should receive attention during the application and so that some common problems encountered during the application can be addressed.

## 1 Application form for the special review of innovative medical devices

Complete the form as required and upload the correctly signed and sealed application form.

The product name shall meet the Rules for Generic Naming of Medical Devices and related documents. The content filled in for performance, structure and composition, the principal working principle or mechanism of action, and the intended use shall reflect all important information on the characteristics of the product, shall be concise, and shall use standard professional language that is not readily ambiguous. The information in the application form (including the remarks) shall be complete and true, and the reasons for recusal of experts shall be specific.

## 2 Evidence of the applicant’s enterprise qualification

A domestic applicant shall submit a copy of the business licence.

An overseas applicant shall submit a document of enterprise qualification issued by the medical-device authority of the country (region) where the overseas applicant is registered. The document shall bear the seal of the issuing authority or be notarised by a local notary.

The electronic directory states the overseas sentence as: an overseas applicant shall submit a document of enterprise qualification issued by the medical-device authority of the country (region) where the applicant is registered.

## 3 Intellectual property in the product, and evidence

This level has no content.

### 3.1 Explanation of the intellectual property in the core technology of the product

Provide an explanation of the intellectual property in the core technology of the product. If there is more than one invention patent, it is suggested that the patent name, the patentee, and the patent status be shown in a list.

### 3.2 Evidence of the related intellectual property

(1) Where the applicant has obtained a Chinese invention patent, provide copies, signed and sealed by the applicant, of the patent certificate, the claims, and the description, and the original of the duplicate of the patent register issued by the patent authority. The date of the application for special review shall be no more than five years after the date of the patent grant announcement.

(2) Where the applicant has lawfully obtained, by assignment, the right to use a Chinese invention patent, in addition to copies of the patent certificate, the claims, the description, and the duplicate of the patent register held by the patentee, the original of the Recordal Certificate of the Patent Licensing Contract issued by the patent authority shall also be provided. The date of the application for special review shall be no more than five years after the date of the patent grant announcement.

(3) Where an invention-patent application has been published by the patent administration department of the State Council and has not yet been granted, provide copies, signed and sealed by the applicant, of the documents showing that the invention-patent application has been published (such as the notice of publication of the invention-patent application, the notice of publication and entry into substantive examination, or the notice of entry into substantive examination) and copies of the published claims and description. A search report issued by the Patent Search and Consultation Center of the China National Intellectual Property Administration shall state that the core technical solution of the product has novelty and inventiveness. If, during examination, the claims and the description are amended at the request of the patent examination department, the amended text shall be submitted. If the patentee changes, submit evidence issued by the patent authority, such as a copy of the notice that the formalities are in order.

## 4 Summary of the development process and results

Summarise the basis on which the development was undertaken and the laboratory studies, animal studies (if any), and clinical studies and results (if any) that have been carried out. Submit a comprehensive report of the product development, including design input, design verification, and design output.

The title in the guide is “Summary of the product development process and results”.

## 5 Product technical documents

This level has no content. The guide: the product technical documents shall at least include the following three items.

### 5.1 Scope of application / intended use

(1) The scope of application, or the treatment, diagnosis, or other purpose that meets the definition in Article 76 of the Regulations on the Supervision and Administration of Medical Devices, shall be stated, and the stage of care to which it applies (such as post-treatment monitoring or rehabilitation) shall be described.

(2) State whether the product is for single use or for repeated use.

(3) State the devices intended to be used in combination with it (if applicable).

(4) Information on the target patient population (such as adults, children, or neonates), information on patient-selection criteria, and the parameters and factors to be considered during use.

### 5.2 Working principle or mechanism of action

Describe in detail the working principle or mechanism of action by which the product achieves its scope of application / intended use, and provide the related basic research materials.

### 5.3 Principal technical specifications and the basis on which they were determined

State the principal technical specifications and the basis on which they were determined, the specification requirements for the principal raw materials and critical components, the principal manufacturing process and the flow chart, and the test methods for the principal technical specifications.

## 6 Evidence of the innovation of the product

This level has no content. The guide: the evidence of the innovation of the product shall at least include the following items.

### 6.1 Academic papers, monographs, and document reviews, published in core domestic journals or authoritative foreign journals, that fully demonstrate the clinical value of the product

Literature on this product may be provided, and literature on similar products marketed abroad may also be provided.

### 6.2 Analysis and comparison of the use of similar products already marketed in China or abroad

(1) Provide an account of the search for similar products already marketed in China. It shall generally include the databases searched, the search date, the keywords, and the results retrieved for each keyword, and shall analyse how the medical device under application differs from similar products already marketed (if any) in working principle or mechanism of action.

(2) Provide an account of the use of similar products already marketed abroad. Provide comparative analysis supporting that the product is at the international leading level in technology (if any).

### 6.3 The innovative content of the product and its significant value in clinical use

(1) Summary of the innovation of the product

Set out the innovative content, and discuss how the innovation makes the medical device under application a fundamental improvement, in safety, effectiveness, and economy, over existing products or treatments, and gives it significant clinical value.

(2) Related technical materials supporting that the product is innovative.

## 7 Product risk analysis

1. Based on the results of the risk-management process already carried out for the product.
2. Written with reference to the relevant requirements of YY/T 0316, Medical devices — Application of risk management to medical devices.

## 8 Instructions for use (specimen)

They shall meet the relevant requirements of the Provisions on the Administration of Medical Device Instructions for Use and Labelling (CFDA Order No. 6).

## 9 Other materials showing that the product meets Article 2 of the Special Approval Procedure for Innovative Medical Devices

The directory title uses “Special Approval Procedure for Innovative Medical Devices”. The title in the guide is: other materials showing that the product meets Article 2 of the Special Review Procedure for Innovative Medical Devices.

If the product or its core technology has received a national or provincial/ministerial science and technology award, state that and submit copies of the related evidence.

## 10 Materials related to the agent

This level has no content.

### 10.1 Letter of authorisation for the agent

The letter by which an overseas applicant entrusts an agent, or its office in China, to handle the application for special review of an innovative medical device.

### 10.2 Agent’s letter of commitment

The letter of commitment of the agent, or of the applicant’s office in China.

### 10.3 The agent’s business licence, or the registration certificate of the applicant’s office in China

The electronic directory has only this name and no further requirement. The guide lists the same sentence among the documents to be submitted.

The guide: an overseas applicant shall entrust an enterprise legal person in China as agent, or have its office in China submit the application, and shall submit the letter of authorisation, the letter of commitment, and the business licence or the registration certificate of the office.

## 11 Self-guarantee declaration that the materials submitted are true

For a domestic product, the declaration is issued by the applicant. For an imported product, the declaration is issued separately by the applicant and the agent.

## 12 Other

For a further application for the special review of an innovative medical device, provide the acceptance number and the review result of each previous application, and submit an explanation of the changes to the product and of how the application materials have been completed. If the original of an application document was submitted with a previous application for special review, a copy signed and sealed by the applicant may be provided, and the applicant shall issue a document stating the acceptance number and the location of the dossier in which that original is held.

## Format and other requirements in the guide

Format of the application materials:

(1) The application materials shall be arranged in the order of Part I of this guide and bound.

(2) There shall be a table of contents of the materials submitted, including the first-level and second-level headings. The materials corresponding to each second-level heading shall be paginated separately.

(3) Documents written by the applicant shall be printed on A4 paper, in a type size suitable for reading.

(4) Where a copy is used, the copy shall be clear and consistent with the original. Colour pictures and charts shall be provided as colour copies.

Other:

(1) The applicant shall complete every item of the Application Form for the Special Review of Innovative Medical Devices truthfully.

(2) Unless otherwise stated, the application materials for a domestic innovation special review shall be originals and shall be signed and sealed by the applicant. “Signed and sealed” means: the enterprise seal, or the signature of its legal representative or responsible person together with the enterprise seal.

(3) Unless otherwise stated, the original-language materials for an imported innovation special review shall be signed and sealed by the applicant, and the Chinese materials shall be signed and sealed by the agent. “Signed and sealed” for the original-language materials means: the signature of the legal representative or responsible person of the applicant, or that signature together with the seal of the organisation, and a notarial certificate issued by a notary in the place where the applicant is located shall be submitted. “Signed and sealed” for the Chinese materials means: the agent’s official seal, or the signature of its legal representative or responsible person together with the official seal.

(4) The application materials shall be in Chinese. Where the original is in a foreign language, there shall be a Chinese translation.

(5) The requirement for a further application is the same as item 12 of the electronic directory.

(6) At the time of application, the applicant shall also submit the complete electronic documents of the application materials and a declaration that the electronic version is consistent with the paper version. The electronic files shall be submitted in PDF on a USB drive.
