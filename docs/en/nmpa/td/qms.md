# Chapter 6: Quality management system documents

**Source:** NMPA Announcement No. 121 of 2021, Annex 5, Chapter 6  
**Electronic directories:** domestic Class III registration dossier; imported Class II and Class III registration dossier

The electronic directories divide this chapter into Chapter 6A, Quality management system documents, and Chapter 6B, Quality management system information for the device under submission. Section titles follow the folder names. The text under each section is a close translation of the official Chinese wording in Annex 5. Where a directory states the same point differently, that wording is included. A folder that has a name and no further official text is listed by name only.

## Chapter 6A Quality management system documents

### CH6A.1 Application summary letter

The applicant shall undertake that a corresponding quality management system has been established as required by the related regulations, and that a quality management system inspection may be accepted at any time.

The imported Class II and Class III directory also states: documents are filed under the headings below.

### CH6A.2 Table of contents

The submitted dossier shall include a table of contents, covering every heading and subheading of this chapter, and shall state the contents number, the contents title, applicability, the uploaded file name, and the page numbers of the uploaded file, and shall give the page number of each item. Applicability shall state whether a CR folder applies.

Annex 5: the table of contents shall include every heading and subheading of this chapter and the page number of each item.

### CH6A.3 Regulatory information

This level has no content of its own. Documents are filed under the headings below.

#### CH6A.3.1 Product description information

A brief description of the working principle of the device and of the overall manufacturing process.

#### CH6A.3.2 General manufacturing information

Provide every address and the contact information for manufacture of the device or of its components.

Where applicable, provide the name and address of every important supplier for outsourced manufacture, manufacture of important components or raw materials (such as animal tissue and medicinal products), critical processes, and sterilisation.

#### CH6A.3.3 Other forms

The domestic Class III directory and the imported Class II and Class III directory have only this folder name and no further requirement.

### CH6A.4 Quality management system procedures

The high-level quality management system procedures used to establish and maintain the quality management system, including the quality manual, the quality policy, the quality objectives, and the procedures for control of documents and records.

### CH6A.5 Management responsibility procedures

Procedures that, by setting out the quality policy, planning, responsibilities, authority and communication, and management review, form the management-assurance documents for establishing and maintaining the quality management system.

The title in Annex 5 is “Management duty procedures”.

### CH6A.6 Resource management procedures

Procedures that form the documents for supplying sufficient resources (including human resources, infrastructure, and the work environment) to implement and maintain the quality management system.

### CH6A.7 Product realisation procedures

High-level product-realisation procedures, such as procedures that describe planning and customer-related processes.

#### CH6A.7.1 Design and development procedures

Procedures that form the documents for a systematic and controlled development process for product design, from the start of the project through design transfer.

#### CH6A.7.2 Purchasing procedures

Procedures that form the documents for purchased products and services that meet the established quality and/or product technical parameters.

#### CH6A.7.3 Production and service control procedures

Procedures that form the documents for production and service activities under controlled conditions. These procedures address matters such as control of product cleanliness and contamination, installation and service activities, process validation, and identification and traceability.

#### CH6A.7.4 Control of monitoring and measuring devices

Procedures that form the documents showing that the monitoring and measuring equipment used in the operation of the quality management system is controlled and continues to meet the established requirements.

### CH6A.8 Measurement, analysis, and improvement procedures

Procedures that form the documents on how to monitor, measure, analyse, and improve, so as to ensure conformity of the product and of the quality management system and to maintain the effectiveness of the quality management system.

The title in Annex 5 is “Measurement, analysis, and improvement procedures of the quality management system”.

### CH6A.9 Other quality system procedures

Other information that is not covered above but is important to this submission.

The title in Annex 5 is “Other quality system procedure information”.

## Chapter 6B Quality management system information for the device under submission

### CH6B.1 Table of contents

The submitted dossier shall include a table of contents, covering every heading and subheading of this chapter, and shall state the contents number, the contents title, applicability, the uploaded file name, and the page numbers of the uploaded file, and shall give the page number of each item. Applicability shall state whether a CR folder applies.

### CH6B.2 Quality management system information

Under the quality management system procedures, the applicant shall form the related quality management system documents and records. The following shall be submitted and will be examined at the quality management system inspection.

1. The applicant’s basic-information form.
2. The applicant’s organisation chart.
3. The general layout of the manufacturing site and the layout of the production areas.
4. Where the production process has a cleanliness requirement, a copy of the environmental test report issued by a qualified testing institution (with the floor plan attached).
5. The production-process flow chart, which shall mark the principal control points and items and the source and quality-control method for the principal raw materials and purchased parts.
6. A list of the principal production equipment and inspection equipment (including the equipment required for incoming inspection, in-process inspection, and final release inspection; where production is under clean conditions, the environmental monitoring equipment shall also be provided).
7. The quality management system self-inspection report.
8. Where applicable, a comparison of the product proposed for inspection with a product that has previously passed inspection, as to production conditions, production process, and similar matters.

Annex 5 titles this section “Quality management system inspection documents”, and places “where the production process has a cleanliness requirement” before item 4 and “where applicable” before item 8.
