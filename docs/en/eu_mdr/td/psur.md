# Periodic Safety Update Report (PSUR)

**MDR Reference:** Article 86; Annex III  
**BPG Rev.4 Reference:** Section 7.2 (pp. 90-92)  
**Document Code:** AIII-PSUR  
**Responsible Party:** CRO  
**Service Mode:** Write only

## Applicability

- **Required for:** Class IIa, IIb, and III devices
- **Update frequency (per Article 86):**
  - Class IIa: at least every 2 years
  - Class IIb / Class III: at least annually
- **Class I devices:** PMS Report per Article 85 (not PSUR)

## Requirements Checklist

### Content (per Article 86 and Annex III)

- [ ] Summary of PMS data analysis for the reporting period
- [ ] Conclusions from benefit-risk assessment based on PMS data
- [ ] Main findings from PMCF activities
- [ ] Volume of sales data (units sold) and estimation of usage population
- [ ] Summary of complaints (categorised by type and frequency)
- [ ] Serious incidents and FSCAs reported during period
- [ ] Trend analysis against PMS Plan indicators and thresholds
- [ ] Actions taken as result of PMS findings (CAPAs, design changes, labelling updates)
- [ ] Conclusions and recommendations:
  - [ ] Risk-benefit ratio remains acceptable
  - [ ] Actions to optimise safety and performance
  - [ ] Any necessary updates to TD (CER, RMR, IFU, GSPR, etc.)
- [ ] Reference to PMCF evaluation report findings
- [ ] Updates to SSCP triggered (if applicable)

### Lifecycle Integration

- [ ] PSUR conclusions reflected in next CER update
- [ ] PSUR findings feed back into risk management file
- [ ] PSUR triggers PMS Plan updates where needed
- [ ] Alignment with SSCP annual review
- [ ] Alignment with NB surveillance schedule

### Submission

- [ ] Uploaded to EUDAMED (when functional)
- [ ] Available to NB for assessment
- [ ] In absence of EUDAMED: follow NB-specific submission arrangements (per MDCG 2021-1)

## Common Pitfalls (from BPG Rev.4)

- PSUR update frequency not per MDR Article 86 requirements
- Incomplete trend analysis or missing connection to PMS Plan indicators
- Benefits section is a re-statement of CER rather than analysis of new post-market data
- Actions taken not clearly linked to specific PMS findings
- Serious incident analysis lacking root cause investigation
- PSUR not aligned with PMCF report and SSCP updates

## Dependency

PSUR is a **Phase 5** (post-certification lifecycle) deliverable:
- Depends on **PMS Plan** (defines data collection scope)
- Incorporates **PMCF Report** findings
- Triggers updates to **CER**, **RMR**, **IFU**, **SSCP**
- Feeds into NB annual surveillance review

## Notes for CRO

- PSUR is a recurring annual/biannual service -- important for ongoing contract revenue
- First PSUR may have limited data (acceptable if PMS system is recently established)
- Sales data must be obtained from client -- build data collection process early
- End of obligation to update PSUR: see [MDCG 2022-21](/eu_mdr/mdcg/mdcg-2022-21) for guidance
- NB evaluates PSUR as part of surveillance -- quality directly impacts certification maintenance
